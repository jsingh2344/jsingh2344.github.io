Dreams

I dream of listening to Blonde. It’s seven pm and mid August, so the sun has dropped enough to light everything in orange. I’m somewhere on a dusty plateau in the Winds, where there are acres of treeless meadow going north and south. I’m tired, I’ve been moving all day, but I’ve made it to the point where my body is relaxing into the exhaustion. I’ll finish the night listening to a podcast. Right now, the plateau is warm and breezy, and the moment needs music.

Looking west one of the boundary ranges is glowing. I ran out of water a few hours ago. The sun dips in and out of a cloud. In the distance I can see the Tetons. I have a habit of flipping to a memory on one of those more familiar mountains, the same time of evening, and I feel connected to the younger self that made the same decision to play in the hills that day.

I dream of looking up at the north face. The Interstellar soundtrack is on, and it’s 5 in the morning, so the Grand is lit by the stars. I’m breathing steady and deep.

I have the strength to remain calm. The climbing is simple. Tension on and off, the air is icy, the sky lightens to dark blue. A car revs on the park road and the hum blends with the wind. I step around a ledge and look down.

Halfway up I find a rhythm. I no longer feel the need to be strong. I’ll be sad to reach the top, see the features I expect to see, and take the easy way down.

I dream of the fight. This time I’ve fucked up, and I need something extra to make it out. I dig my axe in and brace against the snow, the wind, the ice. It’s hard to see.

Little things will not hold up. I want it to be windier, colder, harder. I lean into my narcissism and my arrogance. I feel energy from nowhere; like the kid on Mount Owen I slam my fist into the snow over and over until I nearly break my hand.

I dream of dancing home. My day is finished and I’m back on the trail. Late afternoon is warm and dusty, and I’m high up enough that I’ll basically have the park to myself. There’s a mental satisfaction that I cannot replicate anywhere else.

In my memory, and my imagination, this is one of the simplest places to be. So I turn on a beat and enjoy the moment.
