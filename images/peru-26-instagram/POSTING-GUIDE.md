# Peru 2026 Instagram carousel

## Use this folder

Upload the eight JPEGs in `ready-to-post` in filename order, from `01` through `08`.

All eight files are already 1080 x 1350 pixels (Instagram-friendly 4:5 portrait canvases). The near-black borders are intentional. They preserve each original composition, make landscape and portrait photographs behave consistently in one carousel, and suit the dark blue/alpenglow palette better than white borders.

Do not crop them again and do not add an Instagram filter.

## How to post from a phone

1. Move the entire `ready-to-post` folder to the phone with AirDrop, Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or a cable.
2. In Instagram, start a new **Post**, choose **Select multiple**, and select `01` through `08` in order.
3. If Instagram initially shows a square crop, use the expand/aspect-ratio control on the first image so the post stays 4:5. Check every slide before continuing.
4. On the filter/edit screen, leave the filter at **Normal/None** and do not change brightness, contrast, or crop.
5. Add the caption and a location only if you want one. Use the most precise location you are comfortable making public.
6. In Instagram's media-quality settings, turn on the highest-quality upload option if it is available. Upload over a solid Wi-Fi connection.
7. Before sharing, confirm the first slide is the flag portrait and the final slide is the distant range.

Instagram changes menu labels occasionally, but the flow is always: new post, multiple selection, verify crop, caption, share.

## Curation and edit notes

The source list came directly from `peru_26.html`. The page names ten files, but `DSC_2269.jpg` and `DSC_2276.jpg` are missing from the repository, so the carousel uses the eight available webpage photographs.

Each selected image received an AI-assisted, restrained editorial pass tailored to its content: controlled snow highlights, slightly opened shadows, reduced cyan where needed, preserved blue-hour mood, natural alpenglow, modest detail cleanup, and no added typography or decorative effects. The person frames used identity-preservation instructions; the flag frame additionally required the visible wording, logo, folds, and pose to remain intact. If exact documentary fidelity at the smallest detail matters, compare an edited frame with its original before posting.

The corresponding uncropped edited PNGs are in `full-frame-edits`. Originals in `images/peru-photos` were not changed.
